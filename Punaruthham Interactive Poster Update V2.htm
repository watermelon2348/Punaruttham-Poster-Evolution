01 / OUR JOURNEY

Giving the past a future.

From a fading inscription to a non-contact robotic preservation system.

1stWRO INDIA NATIONALS2026 · International finalists
Concept illustration

THE QUESTION THAT STARTED IT

What if we could preserve a story before it disappears?

Weathering, damage and difficult access can hide the words carved into our heritage. We capture what remains, assist interpretation and preserve the evidence.

Non-contactExpert-ledAccessible

ENGINEERING DESIGN PROCESS

Explore every stage
01

Define the problem

Protect the original. Preserve its meaning.

Our challenge combines three needs: make worn inscriptions easier to see, reach them consistently and keep their information accessible.

DESIGN REQUIREMENT

Capture surviving detail without touching the inscription.

↻Every test informs the next iteration.

01 Define the problem. Capture surviving detail without touching the inscription.

02 Conduct research. Combine complementary views and preserve the original capture.

03 Brainstorm & conceptualise. A mobile, modular rover brings capture and preservation into one workflow.

04 Create a prototype. Separate imaging, motion, processing, power and records into clear functions.

05 Select & finalise. Keep heavy processing on the laptop and bounded movement on the controller.

06 Analyse our work. Compare raw and processed evidence, then inspect uncertainty and failure cases.

07 Improve Punaruttham. Use each observation to improve reliability, clarity and access.
